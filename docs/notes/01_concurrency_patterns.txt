StudySync Academic Notes: Concurrency Patterns & Synchronization Primitives
=============================================================================
Subject: CS-301 Operating Systems & Concurrent Systems
Date: 2026-09-16

1. Mutex (Mutual Exclusion):
   - Only one thread can acquire the lock at a given time.
   - Prevents race conditions during critical section access.

2. Counting Semaphores:
   - Maintains an internal counter representing available resource tokens.
   - wait() / P() decrements the counter, signal() / V() increments.

3. Read-Write Locks:
   - Multiple concurrent readers permitted.
   - Writers acquire exclusive access to avoid dirty reads.

4. Deadlock Prevention Conditions:
   - Mutual Exclusion
   - Hold and Wait
   - No Preemption
   - Circular Wait
