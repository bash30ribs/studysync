StudySync Academic Notes: Cryptography Primitives & Key Exchange
=============================================================================
Subject: CS-340 Applied Cryptography & Security
Date: 2026-09-16

1. Symmetric Encryption:
   - AES-GCM (Authenticated Encryption with Associated Data).
   - High throughput, constant memory footprint.

2. Asymmetric Key Exchange:
   - RSA & Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH / Curve25519).
   - Digital Signatures via Ed25519 / ECDSA.

3. Cryptographic Hashes:
   - SHA-256, SHA-3, BLAKE3.
   - Collision resistance, preimage resistance, avalanche effect.
